- **A writer that cannot read `sys/compat` no longer writes past the
  freshness horizon unless every failed read was refused as access denied.**
  A process whose `sys/compat` reads all failed with a 5xx, a timeout or
  throttling now refuses at once (`view_stale`) instead of writing to buckets
  whose `compat_epoch` is 0 indefinitely; only a view that saw no record and
  whose failures were all access denied keeps that exemption (ADR-4233
  decision 3). A first touch that would provision a new segment family under
  a stale view is refused as retryable (HTTP 503, gRPC `UNAVAILABLE`), counted
  under `ravel_compat_publish_refused_total{reason="view_stale"}`, and writes
  no provisioning record. Alert ticks with no publish record are held to the
  default one-hour publish lifetime. The fold now names key-index leaf
  version 2 as the version it writes. Compaction counts a bucket held for an
  unknown section kind apart from one held for being outside the reader
  window, and a `--mode maintain` server renders it as
  `ravel_maintain_buckets_held_unknown_section_total`; maintenance publish
  refusals render under their own `reason` labels.
