- **`ravel-cli load` words the `--target-bytes` bound as an absolute bound and
  names a clamped derived target as clamped in its no-effect warning** (issue
  #3376). A `--target-bytes` above 360,000,000,000 is now refused with
  `--target-bytes must be at most 360000000000 (one hour of encode at
  100000000 bytes per second, the allowance the ack deadline adds when
  --ack-margin is unset); N was given`; the refusal applies with
  `--ack-margin` given too, which the old wording did not say. When a derived
  target clamped to 360,000,000,000 lays the objects out as `1` would, the
  `target warning` reads `the derived flush target of 360000000000 bytes
  (250000 rows at the first batch's estimated <N> bytes per row, clamped to
  the 360000000000 maximum)` instead of describing the target as 250,000 rows
  times the per-row estimate, which it is not. The same warning now says its
  per-shard slice explanation holds at an even split of each batch.
