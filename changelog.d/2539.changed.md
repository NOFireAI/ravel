- **A query audit record that finds the pipeline idle is written at once**
  (ADR-2509 decision 2, ADR-0062 amendment, issue #2539). The query-audit
  pipeline used to hold a record for up to `--audit-max-age` (25 ms by
  default) waiting for others to batch with, so sequential queries paid the
  full wait on every response. A record is now written without waiting when nothing else
  is queued, it was not submitted while a write was in progress, and the
  previous record arrived at least `--audit-max-age` earlier. Traffic arriving
  more often than once per `--audit-max-age` batches as before, and the
  pipeline writes at most twice as many objects as before on any arrival
  pattern. Every query still waits for its audit record to be durable before
  its response is released, and a failed write still fails the query in
  `--audit-mode required`.
