- **A metrics range query charges its query memory budget for what its
  sample fold holds.** The fold under-charged many small series by about a
  quarter of their bytes, over-charged a few long series by about an
  eighth, and kept merge headroom for every series until the query ended.
  It now charges each container by its capacity before it grows, releases
  a refused or failed growth exactly once, and keeps headroom only for a
  series that will merge.
