- **`ravel-cli load --target-bytes` help and the ingest guide state the
  360,000,000,000 bound as an absolute bound** (issue #3382). A given
  `--target-bytes` above it is refused whether or not `--ack-margin` is
  given, and a target derived with the flag unset is clamped to it. The
  load report's geometry carries a new `target_clamped` field, and the clamp
  and its report now read one unrounded figure: a derived target whose
  unrounded product lands exactly on the bound is no longer reported as
  clamped.
