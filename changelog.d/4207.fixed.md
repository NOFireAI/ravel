- **A loopback store caps the derived store GET concurrency at 64.** With
  `--store s3`, `--store gcs` or `--store azure` against a loopback endpoint
  and neither `--store-get-concurrency` nor `--fetch-concurrency` set, the
  derived GET permit count is now `min(max(8, 2 x cores), 64)` instead of
  growing with cores (384 on a 192 vCPU host), because on a loopback store
  384 permits measured slower than 32 (ADR-4203 decision 3). When the cap
  lowers the value, the startup line `performance default resolved
  setting="store_get_concurrency"` reports source `derived-loopback-cap`.
  An explicit `--store-get-concurrency` or `--fetch-concurrency` is used as
  given, `--sql-partition-count` and `--promql-fetch-fanout` derive as
  before, and a remote store is not capped. The loopback `latency-first`
  default is unchanged, but on a loopback host with 128 or more cores and
  no concurrency flag its startup line now reports `precondition_met=false`
  where it reported `true`, since 64 is below the 256 that policy was
  measured at.
