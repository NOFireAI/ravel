- **`ravel_query_sql` no longer serves a row twice across a walk ordered by
  an aggregate or window value** (issue #3749). A statement such as
  `SELECT ts, count(*) AS c FROM samples GROUP BY ts ORDER BY c, ts` used to
  get a cursor, and a row committed after page one changed a group's count
  and moved that group after the cursor, where the next page served it
  again. An `ORDER BY` term computed from other rows (an aggregate, a window
  function such as `count(*) OVER ()` or `row_number() OVER (...)`, or a
  subquery) now carries no cursor: the statement runs as written, a capped
  result is `ok_bounded`, and an `order_by_the_rows_own_columns` next step
  says that paging needs an order on the row's own columns.
