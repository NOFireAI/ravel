- **A grouped aggregate with many groups over more than one SQL partition
  no longer refuses with "the query memory pool is full and spill is
  disabled" on DataFusion 55.2** (issue #2720). DataFusion 55.2 hands a
  grouped aggregate's output out as slices of one materialized batch, and a
  round-robin `RepartitionExec`, a `SortExec` or a `SortPreservingMergeExec`
  charges each slice the size of the whole batch, so the charge grows with
  the number of slices in flight. ClickBench q29 over the `logs` table (100M
  rows, `regexp_replace(Referer)` as the key, `AVG`, `COUNT(*)`, `MIN`,
  `HAVING COUNT(*) > 100000 ORDER BY ... LIMIT 25`) answered on DataFusion
  54.1 with a 1.63 GB query-pool peak at 32 partitions; on 55.2 it was
  refused at every pool size up to 26 GB while the process held 12 GB, and
  answered with `--sql-partition-count 1` at a 1.20 GB peak, at 2
  partitions at 3,908,874,628 and 4,886,093,285 bytes, at 4 at
  5,863,311,942 and at 8 at 9,780,706,250: each an exact multiple (4x, 5x,
  6x, 10x) of the aggregate's 977,218,657-byte output. The SQL session now
  inserts a `CompactSlicesExec` above every grouped aggregate whose output
  feeds one of those operators; it gives each slice buffers of its own, so
  the consumer charges the slice's bytes. It copies each such aggregate
  output once and passes a batch that already owns its buffers through
  untouched. `EXPLAIN` shows the operator and `EXPLAIN ANALYZE` its
  `copied_bytes`, `compacted_batches` and `passed_through_batches` metrics.
  A hash `RepartitionExec`, which rebuilds its batches, is not a consumer of
  this kind and gets no copy. The regression test (the q29 shape over 20,000
  groups in 79 slices, 4 partitions) measures the peak at 6.0x the
  aggregate's output without the operator and 2.2x with it. Upstream:
  apache/datafusion#24704.
