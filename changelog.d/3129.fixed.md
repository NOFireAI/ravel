- **A slice worker that never completes TLS, or whose connection goes silent,
  no longer holds a distributed query until its deadline.** Slice dials on the
  PromQL and SQL lanes, and the federation dial, now bound the TLS handshake
  at 3 s and send HTTP/2 keepalive PINGs every 10 s, closing a connection
  whose PING goes unanswered for 10 s. On a slice lane either failure takes
  the existing re-dispatch and coordinator-local path. A live worker that
  still answers PINGs but is stuck before its first response is still bounded
  only by the query deadline.
