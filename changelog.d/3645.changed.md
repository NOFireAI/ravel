- **`ravel-server` runs eight flush slots per shard by default, up from four**
  (issue #3645). `--max-inflight-flushes-per-tenant` therefore resolves to 7
  when unset. At four, one busy tenant could hold every slot of a shard and
  push its flushes to the 32 MiB backstop, cutting logs ingest at a fixed
  150k lines/s from about 152k stored to about 115k. Set
  `--max-inflight-flushes 4` to keep the old behaviour; `ravel-cli load` is
  unchanged.
