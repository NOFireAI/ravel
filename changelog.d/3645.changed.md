- **`ravel-server` runs eight flush slots per shard by default, up from four**
  (issue #3645). `--max-inflight-flushes-per-tenant` therefore resolves to 7
  when unset. With few slots, one busy tenant spent most of its time at its
  share, so its ordinary flushes were refused until its buffer reached the
  32 MiB backstop. On the logs ingest benchmark at a fixed 150k lines/s
  (measured with the share set to the slot count), four slots stored about
  115k lines/s and eight held about 152k in two of four windows. Set
  `--max-inflight-flushes 4` to keep the old behaviour; `ravel-cli load` is
  unchanged.
