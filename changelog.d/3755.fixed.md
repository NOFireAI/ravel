- **An MCP PromQL range whose last step overflows is refused** (#3755,
  review #1388, epic #1374). `ravel_query_promql` and
  `ravel_analyze_timeseries` computed a range's last step as
  `start + (end - 1 - start) / step * step` unchecked, so a range under the
  step limit whose span does not fit an `i64` (for example
  `-9000000000000000000` to `9000000000000000000` ns with a `2000000s`
  step) panicked in a debug build and reported a wrong instant in a release
  build. The arithmetic is now checked, and such a range fails with
  `invalid_argument` before the backend runs
  (`a_range_whose_last_step_overflows_is_refused_before_the_backend`).
