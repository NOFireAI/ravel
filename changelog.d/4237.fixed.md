- **A compaction claim at an unknown `format_version` is never stolen.**
  Previously an unreadable claim, including one whose `format_version` is 0
  (never written by any build) or above this build's `CLAIM_FORMAT_VERSION`
  (written by a newer build), was stolen once older than the 24 h lease
  ceiling plus jitter, the same as an ordinary unreadable claim. A claim at an
  unknown version is now version-protected (ADR-4233 decision 4, H8): it is
  never stolen or overwritten, at any age, and the bucket's erasure or
  compaction stays pending until an operator deletes the claim object by hand
  or a build that knows the version restarts and reclaims it. The
  `ravel_maintain_claims_skipped_total` counter reports this case under its
  own reason, `version_protected_claim`, instead of folding it into
  `unreadable_claim`, and this process logs the hazard once per claim at
  `warn`, not on every tick.
