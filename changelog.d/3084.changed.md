- **`--cache-bulk-scan-min-bytes <BYTES>` reads large filtered SQL scans as
  bulk scans** (ADR-3072 decision 3a, #3084 amendment). Off by default, which
  keeps the predicate-free rule alone. Set, a `POST /api/v1/sql` statement is
  also a bulk scan when it names one `samples` or `logs` table and no Parquet
  table, its resolved segments sum to at least `BYTES` of object size before
  any read, and its `WHERE` has no equality on a field the tenant indexes in
  KEY_IDX. Its reads then go to the fetcher cache's bulk region
  (`--cache-bulk-share`) instead of the interactive queues, and
  `stats.cache.intent` reports `bulkScan`. Rows returned are unchanged.
