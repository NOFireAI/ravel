- **Store wrappers forward pinned reads to their inner store** (issue
  #2391). Under `--tenant-kms-config` the server's store wrapper did not
  forward them, so a Parquet table read failed there. The same held for the
  bench and simulator wrappers `DelayedGetStore`, `CountingBackend` and
  `SharedStore`. Through each of these, `get_pinned` failed with
  `Unsupported`, and `get_with_pin` and `pin_of` returned an ETag-only pin
  that dropped the inner store's version selector.
