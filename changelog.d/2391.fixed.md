- **`DelayedGetStore`, `CountingBackend`, and `SharedStore` forward pinned
  reads to their inner store** (issue #2391). Previously `get_pinned`,
  `get_with_pin`, and `pin_of` fell back to the trait's refusing default
  through these three bench and simulator wrappers, so a pinned read through
  any of them failed with `Unsupported` instead of reading the pinned
  version. The Parquet reader is the first pinned-read caller, so a bench
  pass over Parquet tables through these wrappers now reads the pinned
  bytes instead of failing or under-reporting its read cost.
