- **`DelayedGetStore`, `CountingBackend`, and `SharedStore` forward pinned
  reads to their inner store** (issue #2391). Previously a `get_pinned`
  through any of these bench and simulator wrappers failed with
  `Unsupported`, and `get_with_pin` and `pin_of` returned an ETag-only pin
  that dropped the inner store's version selector. The Parquet reader is the
  first pinned-read caller, so a bench pass over Parquet tables through
  these wrappers now reads by the full pin instead of failing.
