- **A malformed columnar log batch is refused with a clear error instead of corrupting or crashing** (issue #2460). Mismatched column lengths, a malformed dictionary, a duplicate stream in one batch, or a stream reference past the end of the batch are now rejected up front, at ingest and at the segment writer, instead of panicking, silently misattributing data to the next batch, or being reported as the wrong kind of error.
