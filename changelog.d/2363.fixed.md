- **`ravel-cli` Maintain-credential data writes go out under the tenant's KMS
  key** (issue #2363). `maintain compact-bucket`, `compact-tenant`, `migrate`
  and `catalog fold` take ravel-server's `--tenant-kms-config` flag and route
  their writes through the tenant's key the way the servers do, where before
  they wrote under the bucket's default encryption; Admin control records stay
  under the bucket default by design, and the `commit reconstruct` access-denied
  error now says so.
