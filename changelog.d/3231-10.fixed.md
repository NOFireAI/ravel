- **`CompactSlicesExec` reports the rows and order its TopK pre-filter drops**
  (epic #3231, issue #3517). With the pre-filter on, the exec keeps at most k
  rows of each batch in the TopK's order, but still reported its input's row
  count, an unchanged cardinality and its input's ordering. It now reports a
  cardinality that can only shrink, no maintained input order and no output
  ordering, and an inexact row count of at most k per estimated input batch,
  which `EXPLAIN` with `datafusion.explain.show_statistics` prints on its line
  (`the_topk_prefilter_reports_fewer_rows_and_no_order`). No answer changed:
  the only physical optimizer rule that runs after the exec is placed does not
  read ordering, and the TopK's own sort sits above it.
