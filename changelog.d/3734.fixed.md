- **MCP audit records identify the tool call** (#3734, epic #1374). Every
  audited MCP tool call now records `query.language = mcp:<tool>` instead of
  the engine's language, which moves to `query.engine_language`, and carries
  `query.id`, a UUID v4 minted per call that the tool result also reports as
  `ids.query_id`. All records of one call, such as the two a
  `ravel_search_logs` call with predicates writes, share that id.
  `ravel_find_labels` records its matcher set as `query.text` instead of an
  empty string. `ravel_capabilities` still writes no audit record, and its
  protocol revision list now comes from the transport's own list. Queries
  outside MCP are recorded as before.
