- **`ravel-server --store azure` runs on an Azure Blob Storage container
  (ADR-4158).** `--azure-account` (`RAVEL_AZURE_ACCOUNT`) and
  `--azure-container` (`RAVEL_AZURE_CONTAINER`) are required; the credential
  comes from the `AZURE_*` environment (AKS workload identity, a managed
  identity, or an account key). The store uses the S3 request and connect
  timeouts and the same store metrics, refuses a hierarchical-namespace
  account, and is gated on a qualification record naming
  `az://<account>/<container>`. Under `--store azure` every `--s3-*` and
  `--gcs-*` flag, `--s3-kms-key`, `--tenant-kms-config` and
  `--require-bucket-protection` is refused at startup when given on the
  command line or through its variable, and every `--azure-*` flag is refused
  under the other stores. A Parquet credential profile on Ravel's own account
  and container is refused. `ravel-cli` does not take `--store azure` yet, so
  it cannot write the qualification record for an Azure container.
