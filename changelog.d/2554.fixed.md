- **A log attribute nested deeper than the segment format holds is rejected at
  OTLP admission** (issue #2554). A resource or scope attribute 16 to 99
  kvlists deep used to be admitted and then fail the flush for every request in
  the tenant's batch; it is now rejected with `AttributeTooDeeplyNested` (at
  most 15 kvlists around a value, an array level costing half a kvlist level),
  the same rule the RLOG writer and its `stream_attrs` decoder enforce. The
  `attrs_raw` decoder keeps reading every value written under the older,
  looser bound; its one new limit refuses a value nested more than 128 maps
  and lists deep instead of overflowing the stack, above the 100 levels OTLP
  ever admitted.
