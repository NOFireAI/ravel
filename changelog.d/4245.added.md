- **Maintenance writers follow `sys/compat` (ADR-4233).** With a compat
  policy in `CompactorConfig::compat_policy`, compaction, the erasure
  rewrite, `migrate`, the force 2 re-encode and the query-audit and
  legal-hold writers fix their write versions at run start, before the
  claim and any PUT, and refuse a stale view, a missing record on a fenced
  bucket, an unwritable active version or a publish lifetime above the
  recorded one, counted under
  `ravel_compat_publish_refused_total{family,reason}`. Every PUT attempt,
  retries included, rechecks the view's age; the first publish above the
  baseline raises the bucket's fence before its first PUT; a record PUT is
  abandoned once the recorded publish lifetime has passed. `migrate` refuses
  a target that is not the active version, and the re-encode refuses unless
  `compaction-record` is active at 2. `background_migrate_unit` runs one
  budgeted walk toward the active version. Compaction and `migrate` hold a
  bucket whose input carries section kinds this build does not know, counted
  under `ravel_maintain_buckets_held_unknown_section_total`. No shipping
  binary installs a policy yet (#4246); with none, the unknown-section hold
  is the only change and every other bucket compacts to the same bytes as
  before.
