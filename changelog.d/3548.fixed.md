- **The scrub, `ravel-cli catalog verify` and `ravel-cli load` check a
  snapshot part's tenant before reading it.** A part whose header names
  another tenant is an isolation fault: it counts in
  `ravel_catalog_isolation_breach_total` and fails the read. The scrub logs it
  at `error` and skips that check for the tick; the covering-postings load no
  longer degrades it to "no postings this tick". `catalog verify` and the load's
  coverage check fail with the new error instead of reporting over another
  tenant's entries.
