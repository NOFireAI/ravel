- **The scrub, `catalog verify` and the load's coverage check refuse another
  tenant's snapshot part.** Each now checks a snapshot part header's
  tenant_hash before decoding it, fails with an isolation-breach error naming
  the part and both hashes, and counts it on
  `ravel_catalog_isolation_breach_total`. The scrub's covering-postings load
  used to read past such a part as an unindexed signal; it now logs the error
  at error level, skips that tenant and signal's postings and seal-divergence
  checks for the tick, and counts the breach once on the server's own catalog.
