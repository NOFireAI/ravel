- **`regexp_replace` with a constant pattern compiles its regex once per
  statement instead of once per batch** (epic #3231). The SQL session
  registers a Ravel `regexp_replace` in place of DataFusion's built-in
  (`crates/ravel-sql/src/session.rs`, `register_udf(regexp_replace_udf())`)
  that caches the compiled pattern per (pattern, flags), at most 64 entries,
  for the session's lifetime (`one_scalar_pattern_compiles_once_across_batches`,
  `flags_are_part_of_the_cache_key`, `cache_is_bounded`). Results are the
  built-in's, including errors and nulls (the `differential` property test);
  a pattern or flags supplied per row still runs the built-in.
