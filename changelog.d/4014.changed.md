- **A SQL statement's in-memory attempt plans at least 16 partitions while
  the partition count is derived.** A high-cardinality `GROUP BY` on a small
  host then fits in less memory and answers without the spill retry more
  often: ClickBench statement 9 on a 2-vCPU, 4 GB host answered in memory 9
  of 9 times at a 2.20 GB process budget, against 0 of 9 at the derived 8.
  The spill retry still plans the derived count. `--sql-partition-count` or
  `--fetch-concurrency` turns the floor off. Startup logs the result as
  `setting="sql_first_attempt_partitions"`.
