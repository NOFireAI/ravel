- **The TypeScript agents SDK records steps and effects on the server**
  (issue #3531). `createHttpTransport` posts each step to `worker/step` and
  each tool result or error to `worker/effects/complete` or
  `worker/effects/fail`, instead of keeping them in an in-memory journal; a
  failed commit with an unsettled attempt is refused by the server's 409
  `effect_in_doubt`, and a 404 `agent_gone` is `AgentGone`, on which the
  worker abandons the turn without retry. The client gains `effects`,
  `effect`, `reconcile` and `destroy` (the last two sent once, never
  retried), and `agent.state()` answers `state: null` for an agent that has
  never committed, in the test harness as on the server. While a turn
  runs, the worker renews its lease in the background with
  `worker/heartbeat`, every third of the lease left, and abandons the turn
  without a commit when a renewal is refused with `OwnershipLost` or
  `AgentGone`. The `journal` option of `createHttpTransport` is deprecated
  and ignored, and passing one logs a warning once; it will be removed. The
  transport's `release` method is removed.
