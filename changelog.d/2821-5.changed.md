- **MetricsBench workload and corpus move to format version 2** (issue
  #2821). Every profile in `benchmarks/metrics/workload.json` declares a
  `range_eval` grid (start and end offsets from the newest sample, and a
  step) for the corpus's range queries, and a manifest without it is refused
  naming the missing block. The generator's out-of-order sample now lands one
  and a half scrape intervals early, between two scrape points, so it never
  repeats a timestamp its series already carries; the ci stream digest
  changes with it. The corpus repairs two join queries that could not answer
  on the generated data (a many-to-many `group_left` and an unreachable rate
  threshold), marks the two native-histogram queries intended-empty until
  native histograms reach the wire, and gains a gate against the manifest:
  every selector matches a generated series, every `group_left` key is unique
  on its one side, and every range query's window plus lookback fits its
  profile.
