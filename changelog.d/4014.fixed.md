- A `COUNT(DISTINCT)` or group-only aggregate over a capacity-accounted
  16-byte group table now reserves ahead only the growth its hash table can
  allocate next, instead of five times its last table step, and a
  `COUNT(DISTINCT)` that keeps a hash set per group reserves that set's next
  doubling. ClickBench statement 9's envelope on a 4 GB host falls from
  293,601,280 to 5,961,852 bytes on the figures issue #4014 reports (ADR-1170,

  from about 34 MB to about 322 MB on those same figures.
