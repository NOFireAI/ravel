- A SQL statement refused for memory now retries through a hash exchange and the bucketed final aggregate when its final aggregate has fixed-width keys, with the bucket count and chunk size derived from the statement's memory share and the process's open-file limit; other statements, and any whose derived plan would not fit the open-file budget, keep the sort-merge retry. The retry's log line names `retry_plan` (`bucket` or `sort_merge`), the reason a statement kept the sort-merge retry, and the derived `bits` and `routed_chunk_rows`. Startup logs two more lines, `sql_spill_retry_descriptor_budget` (the soft open-file limit over twice the spill share count) and `sql_spill_retry_modes`.
