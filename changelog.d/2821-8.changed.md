- **MetricsBench corpus moves to format version 3** (issue #2821). The
  `mb_full_range_avg_over_time` query becomes
  `avg(avg_over_time(metricsbench_gauge_cpu_percent[30m]))`: it scans the same
  data but answers one series instead of one per gauge series. On the
  cardinality profile the old answer was 48,400,000 points, above the 256 MiB
  response cap, so no engine's answer could be compared; the new one is 121
  points. The entry discloses the rewrite in `modified`, and a version-2
  corpus file is refused.
- **A MetricsBench corpus entry may declare a ULP tolerance** (issue #2821).
  `tolerance_ulps` is optional, absent means bit-exact, and the runner hands it
  to the comparator. The corpus gate refuses a zero tolerance and a tolerance
  on an entry without a `modified` reason. `mb_full_range_avg_over_time`
  carries 2^20 ULPs, so a different summation order over its 400,000
  cardinality series cannot read as an incorrect answer.
