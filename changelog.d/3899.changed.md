- SQL memory refusals (per-query pool, tenant, process and aggregate reserve-ahead) now keep their leading text and append the asking consumer's name, its `can_spill` flag and the bytes it asked, the three largest holders by consumer name with their bytes, the bytes held by consumers that cannot spill, and the process aggregate envelope bytes. The query pool also reports whether any grow was refused and the peak bytes held by non-spillable consumers, and `SqlConfig::query_pool_spill_mode` builds a pool that triggers spilling `max(64 MiB, 10% of the limit)` below the per-query limit.
