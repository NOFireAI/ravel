- **Hour-sized many-input log buckets that the two-level merge refused now
  merge** (#3943; ADR-3853 correction 2026-11-07). The level-2 fit check
  counted every part of every scratch run as a level-2 input, so a run cut
  into many parts multiplied its price and buckets whose level 2 fits were
  refused, before level 1 or after it. The check now counts runs, one per
  group, and prices each at two blocks. `PredictedLevelTwoFit` names the
  per-input cost, the group size, the runs and the blocks per run, and
  `LevelTwoFit` names the runs and the blocks per run. Published parts are
  unchanged.
