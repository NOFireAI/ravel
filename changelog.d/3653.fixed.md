- **A tenant whose provisioning record cannot be read no longer drives a GET
  of that record on every write.** After a failed read the ingest router, its
  background re-read and the first-write provisioning check each wait 1
  second before reading again, doubling on each consecutive failure up to 30
  seconds. Writes inside that window are refused with `StaleProvisioningView`,
  or routed inside the grace window, as before. Each failed read now logs one
  ERROR line naming the tenant hash, signal, cause and retry delay, and counts
  under the new `ravel_ingest_provisioning_read_failures_total{signal,cause}`.
  The first-write check's WARN no longer claims that ingest continues.
