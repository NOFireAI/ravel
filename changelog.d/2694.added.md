- **`ORDER BY ts LIMIT k` over logs skips blocks and segments that cannot
  reach the result.** Once the TopK holds `k` rows, a logs scan skips every
  block, and never opens any segment, whose minimum `ts` is strictly above the
  TopK's current worst `ts`, and it opens segments in ascending minimum `ts`
  so that threshold tightens early. The answer is unchanged, ties and a second
  sort key included; a descending or non-`ts` leading key skips nothing.
  `stats.pruning` reports the skips as `blocksSkippedByThreshold` and
  `segmentsSkippedByThreshold` (ADR-2677).
