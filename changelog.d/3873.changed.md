- **The two-level RLOG merge runs level 1 on several workers and pipelines
  level-2 encodes** (#3873; ADR-3853 and ADR-0065 amendments 2026-11-06).
  Level 1 merges its groups on `W1` workers, the largest count at or under
  the unit's core share whose groups and run parts still fit level 2, or
  `level1_workers` when it is set. Each worker gets `1 / W1` of the cursor
  budget and resident ceiling and its own memory tracker, and the first
  error or lost claim stops every worker before its next run part. Level 2
  keeps `E` part encodes in flight, derived per unit from the core share
  and the room the final part target leaves under the ceiling, never under
  2. Both figures are on the `rlog two-level merge` log line and in the
  request ledger as `level1_workers` and `encode_builders`. Published parts
  are byte-for-byte unchanged.
