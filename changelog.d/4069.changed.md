- **A retention tombstone in a sealed key-index day no longer re-encodes it.** With the key-index leaf switch on, a fold that finds a retention tombstone in an hour of a sealed snapshot part carrying key-index leaves records the hour in the part's new `retired_hours` (snapshot part ref field 9) instead of re-encoding the part and rebuilding its leaves, and the resolve hides that hour's segments from both key-index tiers; once every hour the part holds is retired, the part leaves HEAD with no PUT. The catalog HEAD format changes for logs, spans and metrics alike: a HEAD is written with `format_version` 2 exactly when some part carries retired hours, and with 1 otherwise. A reader that predates this release refuses a version 2 HEAD instead of serving a retired hour: its resolve falls back to listing, its SQL column-statistics load answers an eligible logs statement with a retryable 503, its maintain deletes nothing for that tenant and signal, and its fold fails closed (docs/catalog-and-mvcc.md, "Mixed versions"). A compaction or erasure rewrite record in a tombstoned hour still re-encodes the part at the fold that lists it. A retired hour's objects stay named by HEAD, and so are not deleted by retention, until the part re-encodes or leaves HEAD. `ravel-cli catalog inspect` prints the HEAD's `format_version` and each part's retired hours, and takes `--json`.
