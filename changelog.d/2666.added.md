- **`ravel_maintain_compactions_in_flight` reports bucket merges in
  progress.** The gauge rises when a merge starts, after its gates and, for a
  run that takes a claim, its claim, before its first catalog or block read,
  and falls when the merge publishes, fails, is abandoned, or its task is
  dropped. The same point logs `compaction merge started` with the bucket's
  commit prefix. The nightly chaos scenario that kills a maintain worker
  mid-compaction now fires its kill on this gauge instead of on the worker's
  log, and its conservation check passes only when the surviving worker
  publishes the bucket the killed worker was merging, waiting up to the
  takeover bound plus one maintenance tick plus a 300 s merge allowance
  after the kill.
