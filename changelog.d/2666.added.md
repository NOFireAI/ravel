- **`ravel_maintain_compactions_in_flight` reports bucket merges in
  progress.** The gauge rises when a merge starts, after its claim and before
  its first input read, and falls when the merge publishes, fails, is
  abandoned, or its task is dropped. The nightly chaos scenario that kills a
  maintain worker mid-compaction now fires its kill on this gauge instead of
  on the worker's log, drives a larger load so a merge lasts long enough to be
  caught, and waits up to the takeover bound for the surviving worker to
  publish before it judges conservation.
