- **A query no longer fails on another query's cache-fetch budget refusal**
  (issue #2248). The read cache collapses concurrent reads of the same range
  into one upstream fetch: a "leader" runs it, "followers" share its result.
  When the leader's own request or byte budget refused the read, every
  follower got a generic, retryable store error, even though the follower's
  own budget was never actually consulted. The leader's closure now reports
  this case as its own typed `CacheFetchError::BudgetRefused`, and a follower
  that receives it retries exactly once, under its own budget, before
  reporting a refusal of its own.
