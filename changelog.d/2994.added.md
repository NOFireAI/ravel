- **The catalog fold writes per-part key-index leaves for logs and spans.**
  For every part it re-encodes, the fold reads each entry's KEY_IDX section
  by ranged GET (a footer suffix, then the section) and writes one `.kidx`
  leaf per (part, field, key slice) before the part, naming them in HEAD.
  An entry it cannot index is listed as uncovered in the leaf instead of
  failing the fold. No query uses the leaves yet. The fold report (and
  `ravel-cli catalog fold`) gains `key_index_ranged_gets`,
  `key_index_bytes_read`, `key_index_leaves_written`,
  `key_index_leaf_bytes_written` and `key_index_uncovered_entries`;
  `ravel-cli catalog inspect` prints each part's `key_index (field 8)` refs.
  The catalog sweep keeps every leaf HEAD names, deletes unreferenced leaves
  past the protection horizon, and reports its deletes by kind as
  `ravel_maintain_catalog_objects_deleted_total{kind="csnap|npost|cstat|kidx|other"}`.
