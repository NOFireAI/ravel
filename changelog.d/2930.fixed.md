- **`AVG(DISTINCT ..)` now averages the distinct values in every statement
  shape.** Before, it returned the plain average of all the rows when the
  statement also had a `DISTINCT` aggregate over a different argument, a
  non-distinct aggregate other than `SUM`, `MIN` or `MAX` (such as `AVG(b)`),
  or a `FILTER` clause on any aggregate in the statement, with or without
  `GROUP BY` (issue #2930). A statement DataFusion rewrites into a group by,
  such as `AVG(DISTINCT a)` alone or beside `COUNT(DISTINCT a)` or `SUM(b)`, was
  already correct and is unchanged. `AVG(DISTINCT ..)` over an integer
  argument stays exact and keeps its partitioned final aggregation.
