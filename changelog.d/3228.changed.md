- **The log and span writers' bloom filter builder stages keys with a faster
  hasher** (#3228). Its staging sets hash with a non-cryptographic Fx-style
  hasher instead of SipHash, which cut the builder's CPU in a benchmark by
  6 to 13% on x86_64 and 12 to 20% on arm64. Keys are still compared by their
  full bytes, and every emitted bloom byte is identical to before.
