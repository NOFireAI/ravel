- **A repeated logs scan alternates the direction it reads its segments in**
  (ADR-0102's #3192 amendment). With a read cache wired, no confirmed
  `ORDER BY ts LIMIT` TopK and no pushed fetch limit, every second execution
  of the same scan (same tenant, segment set, projection and pushed filters)
  publishes its segments in reverse order, so it reads first what the
  previous execution read last, while the cache still holds it. Previously a
  dashboard re-running a statement over more data than the cache held was
  served nothing from it on warm runs. The executor keeps the count per scan
  in a record of at most 4,096 scans, charged to no cache. Rows without an
  `ORDER BY` can come back in a different order between executions. The
  predicate-free whole-segment fast path, the TopK order and uncached scans
  are unchanged.
