- **`ravel-cli load` refuses a `--target-bytes` above 360,000,000,000, and
  every target above 1 now carries its encode allowance in the ack deadline**
  (issue #3289). The bound is the target whose encode allowance at 100,000,000
  bytes per second is one hour; before, a mistyped 7,400,000,000,000 added
  74,000 s of encode allowance, raising every Strict write's ack deadline past
  20 hours. The refusal applies to logs, metrics and spans loads alike. With
  `--ack-margin` given, the margin replaces the allowance. A target at or below one batch's
  estimated per-shard slice used to add no encode time, on the assumption
  that every write then flushed on its own; shards do not split a batch
  evenly, so such a buffer can still accumulate to the target and be released
  by the age trigger, and it now gets the same allowance as any larger target.
  Only `--target-bytes 1` adds nothing.
