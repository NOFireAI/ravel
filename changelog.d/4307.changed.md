- **Startup cache warm-up skips whole-warming logs parts above the plan
  bound.** The whole-part step now reads a logs part the way the query path
  does: whole only when its object size is at or under
  `LogSegmentFetcher::plan_whole_object_bound`, and not at all above it, where
  the query path reads only the part's directories. A part above the bound is
  left to the logs metadata pass, which warms its footer and directories; the
  whole-part step no longer fetches and decodes blocks no plan needs. Such a
  part is counted in a new `parts_above_plan_bound` field on the
  per-(tenant, signal) warm line and the pass-complete line, and a part at or
  under the bound that the per-(tenant, signal) cap drops is counted in a new
  `parts_beyond_cap` field on the same lines, so `parts_warmed`,
  `parts_over_budget`, `parts_beyond_cap` and `parts_above_plan_bound` sum to a
  tenant's logs objects in the warm set. The whole-part
  cap, the metadata share of the cache budget, the warm deadline and `/readyz`
  behaviour are unchanged. A tenant whose parts are all above the bound now
  has only their metadata warmed, so its first column-page reads are paid cold;
  if the logs metadata pass cannot run (an invalid fetch bound), such a part is
  warmed by neither step and the pass logs that at `warn!`.
