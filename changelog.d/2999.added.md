- **`ravel_process_allocator_bytes` reports jemalloc's `mapped` and
  `retained` bytes.** Two new `stat` samples sit beside `allocated`, `active`
  and `resident`, read in the same allocator epoch. `mapped` bounds how much
  of the process's address space is allocator mappings, and `retained` is
  virtual memory the allocator keeps reserved for reuse, which holds no
  physical pages. The existing three series are unchanged.
