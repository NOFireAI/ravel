- **A transient store error no longer stops ravel-server at startup
  (#4325).** The store reads that refuse to start (`sys/qualification`,
  `sys/tenancy`, the `--tenant-kms-config` key-epoch records, the
  `shard_count` provisioning records, `sys/gc` and the `sys/ipcrypt/policy`
  marker) now retry a transient store error with capped, jittered backoff
  until `--startup-store-deadline` (`RAVEL_STARTUP_STORE_DEADLINE`, default
  `15s` from process start), logging one warning per retry. Past the deadline
  the refusal names the read and the attempt count. Every other error refuses
  at once with its previous message, and `0s` restores the old behaviour.
