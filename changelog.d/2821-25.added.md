- **MetricsBench's `--diagnostics` sidecar now carries Ravel's per-query
  object-store cost** (#940, epic #2821, ADR-0927's 2026-11-04 amendment,
  decision 3). A new `ravel_cost` array holds one entry per Ravel
  repetition (`entry`, `pass`, `rep`, `status`, `accounting`, `phases`),
  with `accounting` and `phases` copied as-is from the answer's
  `data.stats.accounting` and `data.stats.phases`. An answer with no
  `data.stats` (a timeout, a transport error, a refusal) still records its
  entry, pass, rep and status, with `accounting` and `phases` both `null`.
  Comparator engines (Prometheus, VictoriaMetrics, Mimir) add nothing to
  `ravel_cost`. A new `ravel_cost_note` field states the two caveats:
  the figures are logical object-store call counts, not billed provider
  requests (decision 8), and each is read from its own response, never
  pooled across queries or processes (decision 9). `report.json` is
  unchanged.
