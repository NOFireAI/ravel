- **Query-worker registry records are authenticated** (issue #1918). The
  query role can write anywhere under `sys/query/workers/`, so any principal
  holding it could add a self-consistent record at a fresh UUID and have
  coordinators dial its endpoint with slices and fragment capabilities. Every
  record now carries a keyed BLAKE3 MAC, under a key derived from
  `--fragment-key-file`, over its process id, endpoint, protocol version and
  liveness stamp. A node admits a sibling only when the MAC verifies under one
  of its keys, reads each record with a bounded 4 KiB range read, and counts
  every skipped record in
  `ravel_query_worker_record_rejects_total{mode,reason}`. A writer without the
  key file can still evict a member by overwriting or replaying its record
  until the member's next beat. Nodes from an earlier release write no MAC:
  during a rolling upgrade an upgraded node skips them as `missing_mac` and
  runs their share of slices elsewhere, while they still admit upgraded nodes.
