- **A principal without the fragment key file can no longer join the
  distributed query live set** (ADR-1918, issue #1918). The query role may
  write anywhere under `sys/query/workers/`, so any holder of it could write a
  self-consistent heartbeat record at a fresh UUID and have every coordinator
  route slices to, and open connections toward, the endpoint it named. Each
  record now carries a `mac`, a keyed BLAKE3 hash over its process id,
  endpoint, protocol version and stamp, under a key derived from the first
  `--fragment-key-file` key; a reader admits a sibling only when the MAC
  verifies under a key derived from any key in its own file. Records are read
  with a range GET of at most 4097 bytes. Records with no MAC, a MAC that does
  not verify, or a body over 4096 bytes are skipped and counted in
  `ravel_query_worker_record_rejects_total{reason}` (`missing_mac`, `bad_mac`,
  `oversize`). The same principal can still evict a member by overwriting or
  replaying its record until the member's next heartbeat. Every node of a
  cluster must now share at least one fragment key, and during a rolling
  upgrade newer coordinators do not route to nodes that predate this change.
