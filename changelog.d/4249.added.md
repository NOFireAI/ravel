- **The retained-data compatibility contract is covered by an executable
  matrix, a frozen corpus and an append-only guard.** ADR-4233 decision 9's
  compatibility matrix and decision 5's rollback table are pinned as named
  tests in `ravel-compat-tests`, which also drives the whole compat lifecycle
  (init, activate, migrate, certify, verify) end to end through the compat
  library functions. A frozen baseline corpus under
  `crates/ravel-compat-tests/corpus/v0.34.0/`, written by the real segment
  codec and a real compaction, is read back with exact recorded results, and
  `scripts/guards/check-compat-corpus-append-only.sh` refuses any edit or
  deletion of a published corpus file. The compat-tests CI lane now also runs
  the `format-drill` window tests of `ravel-segment`, `ravel-logseg`,
  `ravel-rspan` and `ravel-maintain`.
