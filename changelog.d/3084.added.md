- **Whole-tenant SQL scans are admitted to the read cache as bulk reads.**
  The SQL executor tags a statement `bulk_scan` only when it names one signal
  table and no Parquet table, its plan is predicate-free (no `WHERE` or
  `HAVING` clause anywhere, no join and no `LIMIT`), and its window runs from
  the epoch to now; a `samples` statement also needs no segment pruned and
  every segment inside the window, and a `logs` statement no pending erasure
  and every resolved segment inside the window. Its bytes go to the
  fetcher RAM cache's bulk region instead of evicting the interactive working
  set; every other statement, and PromQL, ES|QL, Flight SQL, the startup
  warmup and distributed workers, reads as `interactive`. Results are the same
  under either intent. `ravel-server --cache-bulk-share <fraction>` (default
  `0.5`, strictly between 0 and 1) sets the share of `--cache-max-bytes` the
  region holds against interactive admissions. An interactive admission
  evicts the region's bytes below that share only once the probation and
  main queues are empty, so they stay resident after bulk reads stop. The
  `/api/v1/sql` response's `stats.cache` object reports the statement's
  intent, its RAM-tier hits and served bytes per intent, and
  cache-wide misses and admitted bytes, measured as the movement of
  process-wide counters, so concurrent statements share them. `GET /metrics`
  adds `ravel_cache_intent_hits_total` and
  `ravel_cache_intent_bytes_served_total` with an `intent` label, and leaves
  the label set of every existing `ravel_cache_*` family unchanged.
