- **TLS certificate verification no longer trusts distrusted intermediates.**
  Every shipped binary now builds with rustls-webpki 0.103.16 and webpki-roots
  and webpki-root-certs 1.0.10, which fix RUSTSEC-2026-0382 (certificate
  verification could accept distrusted intermediates) and RUSTSEC-2026-0381
  and RUSTSEC-2026-0383 (the bundled roots trusted distrusted emSign
  intermediates).
