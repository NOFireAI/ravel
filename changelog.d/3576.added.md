- **`ravel-server` reads a principal subject from a token entry or an OIDC
  claim** (issue #3576, ADR-3541 decision 3). A `--tenant-token` or
  `--tenant-token-file` pair's tenant suffix takes one `subject=LABEL` entry,
  a label of 1 to 64 bytes from `A-Z a-z 0-9 . _ @ + -`; a bad label refuses
  startup with an error that names the rule and never the pair. A pair
  without the entry parses as before. `--oidc-subject-claim CLAIM` names the
  string claim read as the subject of a verified OIDC token; a claim that is
  missing, not a string, empty, too long or outside printable non-space
  ASCII leaves the subject absent, still authenticates the request, and
  counts under `ravel_auth_subject_dropped_total{source,reason}`, which
  `/metrics` renders with every pair from zero in the `all`, `gateway` and
  `query` modes. The flag set empty, or without OIDC enabled, refuses
  startup.
- **The agents reconcile and destroy audit records name the caller's
  subject** (issue #3576, ADR-3541). An `agents_reconcile` or
  `agents_destroy` record carries `agents.principal.subject` and
  `agents.principal.subject_source` (`static_token` or `oidc`) when the
  caller's token or claim carries a subject, and neither attribute when it
  does not. No response names the subject, and no request header other
  than the credential sets it.
