- **`ravel-object-store` gains `AzureStore`, a read-write backend for Azure
  Blob Storage (ADR-4158).** The blob's ETag is the version token,
  `CreateIfAbsent` and `CasVersion` are `If-None-Match: *` and `If-Match`,
  and a suffix read is a HEAD followed by an `If-Match` ranged GET, since
  Azure answers a suffix range with the whole blob. Listing runs through its
  own List Blobs client, credentials come from five `AZURE_*` variables
  only, and an account with hierarchical namespace enabled is refused at
  construction. An in-process fake (`azure::fake`, behind `test-support`)
  runs the contract suite in every test run. No binary selects it yet.
