- **parquet moves from 59.3.0 to 59.3.3 for RUSTSEC-2026-0595**, in which
  `parquet` wrote bloom filters as plaintext when writing an encrypted Parquet
  file. Six arrow crates take the same patch step with it (`arrow-array`,
  `arrow-buffer`, `arrow-data`, `arrow-ipc`, `arrow-schema`, `arrow-select`);
  `arrow` and the other arrow crates stay at 59.3.0. Ravel refuses encrypted
  Parquet on read and configures no Parquet encryption. The change is a
  lockfile patch bump.
