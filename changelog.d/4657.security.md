- **parquet and arrow move from 59.3.0 to 59.3.3 for RUSTSEC-2026-0595**,
  in which `parquet` wrote bloom filters as plaintext when writing an
  encrypted Parquet file. The `parquet` crate and six `arrow` crates
  (`arrow-array`, `arrow-buffer`, `arrow-data`, `arrow-ipc`, `arrow-schema`,
  `arrow-select`) take the same patch step. Ravel refuses encrypted Parquet on
  read and configures no Parquet encryption. The change is a lockfile patch
  bump.
