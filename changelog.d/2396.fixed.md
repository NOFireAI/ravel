- **The shipped IAM templates now grant every provisioning-record write, and
  only with the put mode its callers send** (issue #2396). `query.json` and
  `maintain.json` gain a create-only `s3:PutObject` on `t/*/*/prov`, so a
  Query or Maintain process no longer fails at startup, and the maintain tick
  no longer skips a tenant, when that tenant's data predates its provisioning
  record; `maintain.json` also gains a CAS-only grant for the `maintain
  migrate` floor raise. The unconditioned `prov` writes in `gateway.json` and
  `admin.json` become create-only (Gateway) and create-only plus CAS-only
  (Admin), so a compromised Gateway credential can no longer replace an
  existing record (ADR-0055, prov write conditions amendment).
