- **The TypeScript SDK worker stops a turn whose lease renewals keep failing
  past the lease** (issue #3547). Once no renewal has been answered for
  `leaseMs` (a new `serveAgents` option, default 30 s) since the assignment
  or the sending of the last answered renewal, measured on the worker's own
  timers, the handler's signal is aborted with `LeaseExpired` and the turn
  is abandoned with no commit. A step the handler calls afterwards rejects
  with `LeaseExpired` and sends no step record, even when the handler never
  reads its signal; a step whose record was sent before the abort and
  answered after it runs no tool and reports no effect. A tool that throws
  after the abort reports no failure, and a result answered as late after
  the abort sends nothing more. A handler no longer starts an effect after
  another worker may have taken its turn over and been authorized a second
  attempt of the same effect. That holds only when `leaseMs` is at or below
  30000, the server's fixed agents lease (`DEFAULT_AGENTS_LEASE_MS`, which
  no flag sets), so keep it there; issue #3568 tracks carrying the lease
  length on the wire, which would remove the option. A `leaseMs` that is not
  an integer from 1000 to 2147483647 rejects `serveAgents` with
  `InvalidWorkerOption` at startup.
