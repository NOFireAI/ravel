- **The TypeScript SDK worker stops a turn whose lease renewals keep failing
  past the lease** (issue #3547). Once no renewal has been answered for
  `leaseMs` (a new `serveAgents` option, default 30 s) since the assignment
  or the last answered renewal, measured on the worker's own timers, the
  handler's signal is aborted with `LeaseExpired` and the turn is abandoned
  with no commit. A handler no longer runs on after another worker may have
  taken its turn over and been authorized a second attempt of the same
  effect.
