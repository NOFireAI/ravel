- **The TypeScript SDK worker stops a turn whose lease renewals keep failing
  past the lease** (issue #3547). Once no renewal has been answered for
  `leaseMs` (a new `serveAgents` option, default 30 s) since the assignment
  or the sending of the last answered renewal, measured on the worker's own
  timers, the handler's signal is aborted with `LeaseExpired` and the turn
  is abandoned with no commit; a step the handler calls afterwards, or whose
  record is answered afterwards, rejects with `LeaseExpired` and sends no
  step record and runs no tool, even when the handler never reads its
  signal. A handler no longer starts an effect after another worker may
  have taken its turn over and been authorized a second attempt of the same
  effect. That holds only when `leaseMs` is no longer than the server's
  agents lease, `lease_ms` in its agents settings (`AgentsConfig::lease_ms`,
  30 s), so set the two to match; issue #3568 tracks carrying the lease
  length on the wire, which would remove the option. A `leaseMs` that is not
  an integer from 1000 to 2147483647 rejects `serveAgents` with
  `InvalidWorkerOption` at startup.
