- **A logs aggregate over a `LIMIT` subquery no longer answers from column
  statistics of every row.** When a statement loaded column statistics and
  also aggregated a `LIMIT` subquery over one logs object, such as
  `SELECT (SELECT SUM(status) FROM logs), (SELECT SUM(status) FROM (SELECT
  status FROM logs LIMIT 2))`, released versions answered the limited `SUM`,
  `AVG`, `COUNT(*) WHERE col <> lit` or `GROUP BY col` count from the
  statistics of every row instead of the rows the limit keeps: 1408 instead of
  400 in that example. Such an aggregate now scans the limited rows (#4651).
