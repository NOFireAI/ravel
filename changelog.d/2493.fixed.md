- **A large S3 whole-object read under `--store-scheduling` keeps no more
  requests in flight than the permits it holds** (issue #2493). A `GetRange::Full`
  read above the per-request body bound took one scheduler permit and then
  fetched up to four ranges at once below the scheduler. The read still takes
  one permit; once its first response shows ranges remain, it adds as many free
  permits of its class as it has ranges to fetch, up to four, without waiting
  for any, and keeps at most that many ranged requests in flight. A class with
  no spare permit fetches the ranges one at a time. A read that fits in one
  response, and a ranged or suffix read, holds one permit.
