- **A large S3 whole-object read under `--store-scheduling` keeps no more
  requests in flight than the permits it holds** (issue #2493). A `GetRange::Full`
  read above the per-request body bound took one scheduler permit and then
  fetched up to four ranges at once below the scheduler. The read still takes
  one permit; once its first response shows ranges remain, it takes free
  permits of its class until it holds one per range left to fetch, at most
  four, without waiting for any, and keeps no more ranged requests in flight
  than the permits it holds. A class with no spare permit fetches the ranges
  one at a time. A read that fits in one response, and a ranged or suffix
  read, holds one permit.
