- **The ingest object backstop's multiple is now configurable** (issue #3662,
  ADR-2708 D3's 2026-11-02 amendment). `ravel-server` gains
  `--flush-object-backstop-multiple` (env `RAVEL_FLUSH_OBJECT_BACKSTOP_MULTIPLE`),
  threaded into all three ingest pipelines' `IngestConfig` (metrics, logs,
  spans) the same way `--max-inflight-flushes-per-tenant` already is. A
  deferred buffer at its per-tenant flush share still fires its trigger,
  exempt from the share, once its object estimate reaches this many multiples
  of `--target-bytes`, instead of the previous hardcoded 4x
  (`object_backstop_fires_at_four_targets`,
  `object_backstop_fires_at_one_target_with_multiple_one`,
  `object_backstop_multiple_one_spawns_the_deferred_buffer_at_target_bytes`,
  `object_backstop_multiple_four_spawns_the_deferred_buffer_at_four_times_target_bytes`).
  Default is 4, matching today's fixed behaviour exactly; `0` is refused by
  both `ravel_ingest::IngestConfig::validate` and `Cli::validate`
  (`zero_flush_object_backstop_multiple_is_refused`,
  `zero_flush_object_backstop_multiple_fails_validate`), since it would fire
  the backstop on every buffer regardless of size, defeating the per-tenant
  share entirely.
