- **A spans trace_id lookup probes each object's KEY_IDX section and routes
  by shard** (ADR-2707 decisions 5 and 7, issue #2761). A `trace_id =`
  statement skips every object whose shard the trace cannot route to under
  any shard count its ingest hour is scanned under, including a retiring
  count during the scan slack after a reshard, and keeps an object whose
  shard it cannot determine. Each remaining object is probed with at most two
  ranged KEY_IDX reads, and one that does not hold the trace is not read
  further. `stats.pruning` gains `segmentsPrunedByKeyIndex`,
  `blocksPrunedByKeyIndex` and `segmentsPrunedByRouting`, and `stats.phases`
  gains a `keyIndex` entry for the KEY_IDX reads, on both the SQL and the
  PromQL JSON responses. Routing applies to a statement planned on the
  serving node; worker fan-out and Flight SQL's two-call resolve still list
  every shard.
