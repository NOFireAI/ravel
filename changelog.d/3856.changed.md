- **Compaction keeps 32 input reads in flight by default, up from 8**
  (#3856, ADR-3853). `CompactorConfig::input_read_concurrency` and
  `ravel-cli maintain --input-read-concurrency` now default to 32: object
  store reads are latency-bound, and on GCS 32 in flight moved 72 MB/s
  against 16 MB/s at 8. Output bytes are unchanged. An RLOG fetch-per-pass
  merge reserves the 32 largest row groups in flight, about 107 MiB at
  3.5 MB per group against about 27 MiB at 8; set the flag to 8 to keep the
  old bound (`the_default_input_read_concurrency_is_32`,
  `the_default_in_flight_reservation_is_the_32_largest_groups`).
