- **`ravel-cli load` runs the depth flush with an explicit `--target-bytes`.**
  Any target above `1`, given or derived, now closes every shard's buffer
  once its oldest pending batch is `--pipeline-depth` batches old, so a load
  whose shard shares are uneven no longer waits out `--max-flush-delay` on
  every window for a shard whose share never reaches the target; that shard
  writes smaller objects at the window instead. The derived depth gets the
  rows bound's quarter for such a target too, and the stderr geometry line
  ends with `depth flush on` or `depth flush off`. `--target-bytes 1` keeps
  the depth flush off.
