- **The SQL memory pool charges a grouped aggregate ahead of what it asks
  for.** On a SQL `GROUP BY` or an ES|QL `STATS ... BY` that runs on the
  migrated aggregate streams, the
  query, tenant and process budgets are charged 1.25 times the bytes the
  aggregate reserves. The pool also reserves, on the process budget, an
  envelope for the next step of the largest building aggregates, so an
  aggregate whose next step the process budget cannot hold is refused
  before it makes it. The envelope is on by default and sized from the
  plan's aggregate layout: the group table's entry size and the per-group
  bytes of the columns and accumulator states that grow with the group
  count, with five times the largest step as the fallback for a layout it
  does not classify (#3181). `SqlConfig::aggregate_reserve_ahead` sets the
  factor and the envelope; `AggregateReserveAhead::OFF` restores one-to-one
  charging and no envelope.
