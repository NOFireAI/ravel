- **The SQL memory pool charges a grouped aggregate ahead of what it asks
  for.** On a `GROUP BY` that runs on the migrated aggregate streams, the
  query, tenant and process budgets are charged 1.25 times the bytes the
  aggregate reserves, and the process budget also reserves an envelope for
  the next doubling of the largest building aggregates, so an aggregate
  whose next doubling the process budget cannot hold is refused before it
  makes it. `SqlConfig::aggregate_reserve_ahead` sets the factor and the
  envelope; `AggregateReserveAhead::OFF` restores one-to-one charging.
