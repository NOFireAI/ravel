- **The SQL memory pool charges a grouped aggregate ahead of what it asks
  for.** On a `GROUP BY` that runs on the migrated aggregate streams, the
  query, tenant and process budgets are charged 1.25 times the bytes the
  aggregate reserves. The pool can also reserve, on the process budget, an
  envelope for the next doubling of the largest building aggregates, so an
  aggregate whose next doubling the process budget cannot hold is refused
  before it makes it. That envelope is available and off by default, pending
  its sizing from each aggregate's layout (#3181);
  `AggregateReserveAhead::WITH_ENVELOPE` turns it on.
  `SqlConfig::aggregate_reserve_ahead` sets the factor and the envelope;
  `AggregateReserveAhead::OFF` restores one-to-one charging.
