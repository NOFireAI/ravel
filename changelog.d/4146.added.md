- **A metrics part left without a series index says why, field by field.** The
  fold report's new `metrics_series_index_over_cap_parts` and the key-index
  repair report's new `series_index_over_cap_parts` list each part over the
  series-index byte cap or the leaf body ceiling: its leaf bytes and cap, the
  leaf bytes and pairs of `series_label`, `series_class` and `class_objects`,
  and its class and series counts. The warn line carries the same figures.
  Which parts get a series index is unchanged.
