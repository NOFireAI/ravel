- **The fold builds the tail part's key-index leaves incrementally, under a
  pair budget** (#3704, epic #3692, ADR-2707's incremental tail build
  amendment). With `--fold-key-index-leaves` on, the build carries the
  previous tail part's leaves, matched by data object key, and reads KEY_IDX
  sections only for new or uncovered entries; the leaves are byte-identical
  to a full build. It holds at most `--fold-key-index-build-max-pairs`
  (ravel-server) or `--key-index-build-max-pairs` (`ravel-cli catalog fold`)
  pairs per part, default 8,000,000, and builds a larger part in passes over
  disjoint key8 ranges. The fold report, `ravel-cli catalog fold` and
  `/metrics` gain `key_index_sections_read`, `key_index_entries_carried`,
  `key_index_build_passes` and `key_index_leaf_gets`.
