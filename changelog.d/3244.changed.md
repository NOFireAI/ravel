- **SQL over logs loads column statistics only for statements a metadata path
  can answer** (issue #3244). The two-GET column-statistics load now runs for
  one unfiltered `SUM(col)`, `SUM(col + n)` or `AVG(col)`, a `COUNT(*)`
  filtered by a single `col <> literal`, and a `COUNT(*)` grouped by one
  typed attribute column. Inside those shapes a filter conjunct the load
  decision cannot read, such as a `ts` bound written with `TIMESTAMP`
  literals or `NOT (col = n)`, keeps the load. `MIN` and `MAX` are answered
  from the segment stamps and load nothing. Other statements that name a
  typed attribute column, such as several `SUM`s, `COUNT(DISTINCT col)`, a
  `GROUP BY` filtered by `<>` or a two-key `GROUP BY`, no longer pay for a
  load nothing reads.
  Results are unchanged.
