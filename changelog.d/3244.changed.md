- **SQL over logs loads column statistics only for statements a metadata path
  can answer** (issue #3244). The two-GET column-statistics load now runs for
  one unfiltered `COUNT(*)`, `SUM(col)`, `SUM(col + n)` or `AVG(col)`, a
  `COUNT(*)` filtered by a single `col <> literal`, a `COUNT(*)` grouped by one
  declared column with no filter, and any mix of `COUNT(*)`, `MIN` and `MAX`
  with no group key and no filter. Other statements that name a declared
  column, such as several `SUM`s, `COUNT(DISTINCT col)`, a filtered `GROUP BY`
  or a two-key `GROUP BY`, no longer pay for a load nothing reads. Results are
  unchanged.
