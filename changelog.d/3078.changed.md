- **Compound PromQL range queries read each distinct selector once per query.** A range query
  whose top level is an aggregate or binary expression, such as
  `sum by (service_name) (rate(http_requests_total[5m]))`, used to read every
  selector from the query's prefetched series once per grid step; measured on
  issue #3072, a 24 h / 60 s query made 1,441 reads that each walked every
  series whole, and evaluation took 91.7 % of the wall time at 1,000 series.
  The evaluator now keeps each selector's read (keyed by its matchers, its
  float or histogram channel, `offset` and `@`; two selectors that differ in
  range only share a key) in a per-query cache whose first read of a selector
  covers what every grid step asks of that selector, and answers each step,
  including each step of a subquery, by slicing it; it reads again only when
  a step asks for a window outside the cached one, and that read covers the
  cached window too. Results are unchanged.
