- **Compound PromQL range queries fetch each selector once.** A range query
  whose top is an aggregate, a binary expression or a subquery now reads each
  selector from storage once over the grid's window and slices it per step;
  previously a 24 h range at a 60 s step read the store 1,441 times and walked
  every series whole on each read, 77.6 % of the query's time at 1,000 series
  (issue #3072).
