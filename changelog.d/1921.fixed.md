- **One tenant's stalled flush no longer times out a co-resident tenant's
  strict writes.** `--max-inflight-flushes` now defaults to 4 permits per
  shard, and the new `--max-inflight-flushes-per-tenant`
  (`RAVEL_MAX_INFLIGHT_FLUSHES_PER_TENANT`, `spec.gateway.maxInflightFlushesPerTenant`
  on the operator) caps how many one tenant may hold, defaulting to
  `max(1, N - 1)`. A tenant at its share defers its trigger and counts it on
  `ravel_ingest_flush_trigger_deferred_total`. A deferred buffer whose object
  reaches 4x `target_bytes` flushes regardless. A buffered flush granted its
  permit after its pinned ingest hour's end plus the flush lifetime is now
  abandoned without a PUT and counted on
  `ravel_ingest_abandoned_hour_bound_total`, instead of publishing into an
  hour the catalog has already sealed. The server warns at startup when
  `--max-inflight-flushes` is 1.
