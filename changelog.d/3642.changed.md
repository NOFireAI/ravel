- **The whole-segment fast path's serve-if-fits check stops early.** A
  `LIKE` scan whose decoded-set estimate passes 0.95 of
  `--decoded-cache-max-bytes` stops reading segment directories at that
  point, since the verdict can no longer change. Its
  `stats.decodedCache.deferredSetEstimateBytes` is then the sum read so far,
  a lower bound, and `deferredSetEstimatePartial: true` says so. A fast-path
  scan that carries a pushed `LIMIT` and no `ORDER BY` top-k makes no
  estimate at all. A scan that fits reads and reports exactly as before.
