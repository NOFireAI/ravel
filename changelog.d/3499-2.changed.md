- **A logs flush above the write gate's inline floor seals its column pages in
  parallel on the gate** (issue #3499, ADR-3492 decision 7). Each block's page
  jobs after the first take one write-gate permit each on the blocking pool,
  so `ravel_cpu_gate_jobs_total{gate="write",site="log_flush"}` counts one
  job per page job. With no free permit, or a block of one job, the flush
  seals its pages serially under its own permit and counts one job; below the
  floor it stays one inline job. Stored objects are byte-identical to the
  serial encode, strict acks still wait for the data object and its commit
  record, and a failed page job fails the flush with the retryable
  `ShardUnavailable` and counts on `flush_gate_faults`. Measured on the
  `logseg_encode` bench (#3508), the 7.5k-record flush goes from 181.7 ms to
  169.9 ms on 4 threads; ADR-3492's D7 measurement amendment records the
  revised strict-ack prediction.
