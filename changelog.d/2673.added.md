- **The real-S3 performance envelope is committed, and every weekly bench-s3
  run is compared against it.** `bench/baselines/s3-envelope.json` holds five
  runs of the weekly lane at one load point and the min and max of each
  compared figure; `scripts/bench-s3-compare.py` warns on a latency figure past
  the max plus 25 percent, a GET, LIST or byte count past the max plus 10
  percent, and any change in PUTs, accepted points or matched series, and
  refuses a report from a different environment. The lane runs it after every
  run and never fails on it. The cost-model guide's new "Measured envelope"
  section quotes the range, and `scripts/bench/fresh-box.sh` records a tier B
  timing baseline on a fresh EC2 instance that it terminates afterwards.
