- **`ravel-server` installs the `sys/compat` activation policy on every
  writer (ADR-4233 decisions 1, 3 and 4).** On a keyed bucket the server
  builds one `CompatView` per process and hands it to the metrics, log and
  span ingest routers (#4244) and to the maintenance compactor (#4245), so an
  activation this build cannot honour stops ingest at admission and every
  maintenance run, not only the catalog fold and alerting. The ingest routers'
  per-signal refusals now render on `/metrics` under
  `ravel_compat_publish_refused_total{family,reason}` alongside the
  maintenance ones, including the ingest-only `active_version_moved` and
  `no_bucket_view` reasons. On a store that never ran compat init
  (`sys/compat` absent) every writer emits its build's `CURRENT` version, so
  v0.34.0 writes nothing a v0.33.0 binary refuses. A router dropped without
  `shutdown()` now publishes its buffered rows on teardown instead of refusing
  them `no_bucket_view`: the per-tenant compat-epoch cache its shards hold by
  `Arc` outlives the router's switch, so acknowledged rows are not lost
  (docs/consistency-model.md).
