- **The series ids a listing keeps to skip repeated objects no longer crowd
  out other queries.** Concurrent `/api/v1/series`, `/api/v1/labels` and
  `/api/v1/label/{name}/values` reads over metrics share one half of the
  memory budget for those ids, instead of each taking up to half, and a
  listing that cannot keep them counts no budget refusal and evicts no cache.
  The ids are released when the local fetch ends, before federated series
  are added.
