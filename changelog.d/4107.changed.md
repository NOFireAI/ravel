- A two-level RLOG merge now writes its scratch runs at 1,024 records per block instead of 8,192, so level 2 holds a smaller block per run. Published parts are unchanged in an unkeyed bucket and for a keyed stream that does not batch; a keyed stream that ADR-2135 batch-major emission batches by reservation may cut its parts differently, since a run's reservation shrinks with its block target (exact either way, every record conserved). The target is the new `rlog_run_block_target_records` field on `CompactorConfig`, and zero is refused. It has no ravel-cli or ravel-server flag yet; those follow in #4109 and #4110.
