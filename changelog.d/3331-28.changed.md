- **The published `ravel-server` image now carries Ravel Agents** (issue
  #3362). The root `Dockerfile` builds `ravel-server` with
  `--features sql,flight-sql,otap,agents`, the README support matrix row reads
  `yes`, and `docs/internal/development.md` names the same list. The routes
  still mount only with `--agents`, in `--mode all` or `--mode query`. The new
  operator guide, `docs/guides/operations/agents.md`, covers budgets and their
  metrics, capabilities and store grants, projection modes, sweep cost, clock
  assumptions and the runbook for reconciling an effect in doubt, and
  `docs/agents.md` now carries one matrix of every behaviour ADR-3331
  promises, each with its status, TLA+ or kernel row, simulation schedule and
  object-store contract section.
- **Ravel Agents flags in this release.** `--agents-activation-concurrency`
  (64) and `--agents-poll-concurrency` (1024) refuse past their lane with 503
  and take no query permit. `--agents-max-mailbox` (256, 429
  `mailbox_full`), `--agents-max-receipts` (1024, 413 `budget_exceeded`) and
  `--agents-max-timers` (64, 413 `budget_exceeded`) are enforced and named in
  the error body's `cap` field and in `ravel_agents_budget_refusals_total`.
  `--agents-max-agents`, `--agents-max-state-bytes` and
  `--agents-memory-budget` are parsed and reported only; enforcing them is
  deferred (#3393 tracks the memory-budget reservation; #3479 tracks the
  agent-count and state-size limits). `--agents-projection`,
  `--agents-projection-endpoint` (loopback `http://` only),
  `--agents-projection-backlog` and `--agents-projection-payload-tenant`
  choose and bound where committed history is projected. The sweep interval
  and per-pass budgets have no flag.
- **Ravel Agents limitations in this release.** An agent incarnation accepts
  at most `--agents-max-receipts` sends, and its results, events and timers
  lists stop at 32,768 entries each; nothing trims them, and no route
  destroys an agent in this release (#3478). Retention and an opt-in receipt
  window, default off, are #3453. A send writes no wake hint, so with several server
  processes an agent another process could serve is found by the sweep
  within ceil(N/32) passes at 5 s, about 2.5 minutes at 1,000 agents (#3437).
  The projection endpoint sends no credential, so its gateway must run
  `--dev-insecure-tenant-header` on loopback listeners (#3475). Blocked or
  deferred matrix rows: a cancel of an unbound input answers
  `requested: true` (#3404); two admins reconciling one effect are not told
  apart (#3408); a takeover retried after a lost CAS can fence a live turn
  (#3415); the SDK step journal lives in memory (#3416); a poll that loses
  two `BeginTurn` answers hands `OutcomeUnknown` to the worker (#3425). The
  TypeScript SDK keeps steps in worker memory, so the server's effect and
  reconcile routes do not see its steps (#3416).
