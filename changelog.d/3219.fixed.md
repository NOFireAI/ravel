- **A logs scan with no filter that uses BLOOM or POSTINGS no longer fetches
  them.** A ranged open places BLOOM and POSTINGS only when the query carries a
  content arm, a stream-attribute arm, or a prune arm other than a numeric
  range, and the reader reads BLOOM only for a `HasWord` arm or a string
  equality of at most 64 bytes. Previously every ranged open fetched both
  sections, one GET each, so a predicate-free or numeric-range scan now saves
  one GET and the section's stored bytes per section per object.
