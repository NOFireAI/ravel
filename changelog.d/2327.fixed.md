- **A large S3 put under `--store-scheduling` keeps no more requests in
  flight than the permits it holds** (issue #2327). A `put()` above the 16 MiB
  multipart threshold took one scheduler permit and then uploaded up to four
  parts at once below the scheduler. The class handle now takes one permit plus
  as many free ones of its class as the upload can use, without waiting for
  any, and the upload keeps at most that many parts in flight. A class with no
  spare permit uploads the parts one at a time.
