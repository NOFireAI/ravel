- **A `GROUP BY` view memo for the decoded column-block cache** (ADR-3326,
  view memo amendment). `--decoded-cache-view-max-bytes` sets a view budget
  beside `--decoded-cache-max-bytes` for the per-row string views a
  `GROUP BY` over a cached plain-page column reuses; unset it is the
  smallest of a quarter of the cap, 2 GiB and the memory budget less the cap
  and the admission margin, 0 while the decoded cache is off, and `0` turns
  it off.
