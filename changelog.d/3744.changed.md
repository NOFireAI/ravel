- **A refused ingest request keeps its connection for bodies up to 16 MiB.**
  When the server refuses an HTTP/1 ingest request at its head (the in-flight
  limit, the pending-ack limit, or a hold that runs out), it now reads and
  discards up to 16 MiB of body, the request body limit, before answering,
  instead of 1 MiB. A refused 2 MiB OTLP/HTTP body is answered without
  `Connection: close`, so the client does not have to reconnect. Bodies above
  16 MiB are still answered with `Connection: close` and counted under
  `over_cap`.
