- The key-index leaf build now carries a re-encoded part's entries from every previous part with leaves whose hour range overlaps it, so a sealed day that a retention tombstone or a late record changes is rebuilt from its own previous leaves instead of from every entry's sections, and it fetches each previous leaf slice when a build pass first reaches it and drops it once passes have moved past it, instead of holding every previous leaf for the whole build. The fold report, and `ravel-cli catalog fold` in text and `--json`, carries `key_index_carry_peak_held_bytes`, the most previous-leaf bytes the carry held at once while building one part.
