- **A logs declared-key equality routes through the key-index leaves once
  per scan** (#3703, epic #3692, ADR-3692 decisions 4 and 9). Over
  `POST /api/v1/sql`, a logs scan carrying a declared-key equality runs the
  tier-2 router over its ts-relevant segments before the plan pass. A
  segment the part's leaves prove the key absent from is never opened,
  whatever its size; a segment the leaves name blocks for is planned with
  no KEY_IDX read, restricted to those blocks above the whole-object bound;
  every other segment, including one whose leaf failed its GET, its
  checksum or its reservation, takes the tier-1 route unchanged
  (`folded_equality_opens_only_the_holder`,
  `a_failed_leaf_get_leaves_the_part_uncovered`). `stats.keyIndex` and the
  logs entry of `stats.signals` carry the router's ten figures, and the
  `ravel_query_key_index_*` families move under `signal="logs"`. A refused
  parts charge degrades the scan to tier 1 with every figure 0
  (`a_refused_parts_charge_degrades_to_tier_one`). The fold's leaf build and
  `--rebuild-key-index` now list an entry whose RLOG KEY_IDX section is at
  version 1 as uncovered (`version_one_sections_are_uncovered_in_the_leaf`).
  Flight SQL and `EXPLAIN` still probe logs at tier 1.
