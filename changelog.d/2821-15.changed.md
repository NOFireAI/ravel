- **The MetricsBench nightly measures the Ravel it checked out** (issue
  #2821). `metricsbench-nightly.yml` builds the root Dockerfile's `server`
  target from the run's own commit, keeps the image local, and passes it to
  `run.sh` by tag and content Id, instead of a pinned image from an older
  commit, so a regression merged later reaches the next nightly. `run.sh`
  takes the new local-build form `--ravel-image REF --ravel-image-id
  sha256:ID`, refuses it (exit 65) unless `docker image inspect REF` reports
  that Id or when the profile is not `ci`, and stamps `ravel_image.kind`
  `local-build` or `published` in `stamps.json` and the provenance. A
  local-build run is never publishable. The nightly's job timeout rises from
  75 to 140 minutes to cover the image build.
