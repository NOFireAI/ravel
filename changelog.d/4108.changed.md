- Every RLOG compaction, single-level too, now cuts a part at each 2,500,000-record range start and numbers each range's parts from its record share of the part key's 10,000 indexes, so the single-level merge and a two-level merge at any worker count publish the same parts. A unit under 2,500,000 records is one range and publishes the same parts as before; a unit over it publishes different part keys than earlier runs of the same inputs did. A two-level RLOG compaction now runs its second level on several range workers when its memory allows: each worker merges a contiguous block of ranges under its share of the unit ceiling. The level-1 and level-2 worker counts are derived together, each level-2 worker's read side counted against the ceiling; at the server defaults the derivation keeps one worker at each level. Two new typed refusals stop a unit before the part that cannot be numbered is encoded: `PartRangeWindow` when a range needs more parts than its window of indexes holds, and `PartKeySpace`, raised before the first part is encoded, when a unit holds more than 10,000 times the range quantum in records. The first failing level-2 worker stops the others before their next part write. The request report, the `rlog two-level merge` line and the `compaction merge ended` line carry `level2_workers` and `level2_ranges`, the last one also for a run without a request report.
