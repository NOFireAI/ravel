- Every RLOG compaction, single-level too, now cuts a part at each 2,500,000-record range start and numbers each range's parts from its record share of the part key's 10,000 indexes, so the single-level merge and a two-level merge at any worker count cut at the same records and number each range in the same window. They also publish the same keys and bytes, except for a keyed bucket's batch-major streams (ADR-2135 decision 2): batches cut over level-1 runs differ from batches cut over the inputs, and level 1 batches under `merge_cursor_budget_bytes / W1`, so such a stream may cut its parts differently by route and by level-1 worker count (ADR-3853 D3). A unit under 2,500,000 records is one range and publishes the same parts as before; a unit over it publishes different part keys than earlier runs of the same inputs did. A range's window grows with the records it has emitted, so a range holding more than 2,500,000 records gets their share of the indexes. A two-level RLOG compaction now runs its second level on several range workers when its memory allows: each worker merges a contiguous block of ranges under its share of the unit ceiling. The level-1 and level-2 worker counts are derived together, each level-2 worker's read side counted against the ceiling, so the counts depend on the bucket's shape: on the shape of #3961's table at the server defaults the derivation keeps one worker at each level, while on the production shape the tests pin (a core share of 8, a 5 GiB ceiling, a 19,177-input bucket at measured-ratio pricing) it runs 4 level-1 and 5 level-2 workers, and one of each at ceiling pricing. Two new typed refusals stop a unit before the part that cannot be numbered is encoded: `PartRangeWindow` when a range's parts are too small for its window of indexes (under about 1/10,000 of the unit's records each, past the indexes the window holds when the range opens), and `PartKeySpace`, raised before the first part is encoded, when a unit holds more than 10,000 times the range quantum in records. Neither publishes anything nor writes a compaction record, and neither stops the scan or maintain pass: the bucket is counted as refused and held in memory as terminal, its retention still runs, and the shard's later hours still compact. The first failing level-2 worker stops the others before their next part write. The request report, the `rlog two-level merge` line and the `compaction merge ended` line carry `level2_workers` and `level2_ranges`, the last one also for a run without a request report.
