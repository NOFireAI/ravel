- **A `LIKE '%lit%'` logs scan on the striped route plans from the
  directories.** When a logs scan has more partitions than segments, a
  statement whose pushed content holds only `Contains` arms, with no
  stream-attribute arm and only `NumRange` or `NumNotEq` prune arms, now plans
  each object above the whole-object bound from its footer and four
  directories. Its plan phase reads no BLOCKS byte and is no longer counted in
  `plan_full_reads`; previously it read every column of the object's candidate
  blocks to count survivors that no `Contains` arm can reduce.
