- With the key-index leaf switch on, the fold now also seals a snapshot part at every UTC day boundary, so a sealed day keeps its leaves and an advancing fold rebuilds the leaves of the latest UTC day, once those of the day it seals, and those of any sealed day a late record changes, instead of the whole tail; a sealed day carried without a leaf for a declared field (sealed while a metrics erasure was pending, or before the field was declared) gets that field's leaves attached by a later switch-on fold without being re-encoded, and a field that yields no leaf for such a day has its sections read again on every fold; every process folding a tenant must now agree on the switch, and a switch-off fold over a HEAD with leaves reports `key_index_switch_off_over_leaves` and logs a warning. The fold report, and `ravel-cli catalog fold` in text and `--json`, also carries `key_index_pair_budget`, the pair budget the key-index leaf build ran under, and `key_index_field_pairs`, each indexed field's key pairs, a declared field with none included.
