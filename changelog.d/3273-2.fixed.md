- **`ravel-cli load`'s ack deadline covers a target-sized flush.** The
  deadline is now the larger of 60 s and `--max-flush-delay` plus a margin
  of 60 s and the target divided by 100,000,000 bytes per second, the time
  a flush the age trigger released may spend encoding its object before
  the PUT starts. At the stock 1,850,000,000 target that adds 18.5 s:
  138.5 s at the 60 s default delay and 108.5 s at `--max-flush-delay 30s`,
  where the deadline was 120 s and 90 s. `--target-bytes 1` keeps the old
  deadline. The new `--ack-margin <SECONDS>` flag replaces the whole margin,
  and the stderr geometry line ends with `ack deadline <N>s`.
