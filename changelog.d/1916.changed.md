- **An ingest shard whose flushes stay deferred up to the read-side slack now
  refuses new writes with a retryable overload error instead of deferring past
  the slack** (issue #1916). Once a flush deferred at the queued-flush cap has
  waited the deferral cap (3559.8 s at the defaults: the 2 h flush slack less
  `max_flush_lifetime`, the idle flush delay and one flush tick), the shard
  answers strict-mode writes, including those already waiting on the deferred
  flush, with 429 / `RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED` until it opens, and deferred flushes
  now retry oldest first.
