- **A logs exact-key equality probes KEY_IDX on every object above the
  block-range threshold, under the projection break-even too** (issue #3117).
  An object a key is absent from is dropped without being read whole. One
  above the threshold and at or under the whole-object bound (the larger of
  the threshold and the projection break-even) that holds every key, or whose
  probe declines, is read whole. When the scan opens such objects unplanned
  (ADR-2677 decision 2, at least `target_partitions` of them), the plan pass
  reads only each object's tail and KEY_IDX, and the scan reads each object
  it keeps whole in one GET and never a second time, with or without a
  KEY_IDX section. An object
  at or under the threshold is read whole and not probed. A keyed
  statement's `stats.io.dependencyDepth` counts the KEY_IDX prefix and
  bucket reads on every object above the threshold.
