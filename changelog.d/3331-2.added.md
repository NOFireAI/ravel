- **`ravel-server` gains an off-by-default `agents` feature and `--agents`**
  (issue #3331). With the feature, `--agents` mounts
  `GET /api/v1/agents/health` on the public and the mTLS listener of an
  `all` or `query` process; it answers the mode and the settings of
  `--agents-activation-concurrency`, `--agents-poll-concurrency`,
  `--agents-memory-budget` and the five per-agent and per-tenant quota flags,
  which `/metrics` also reports as `ravel_agents_*` gauges. The budgets and
  quotas are parsed and reported only; nothing enforces them yet. A build
  without the feature refuses `--agents`, and `--agents` under `gateway` or
  `maintain` is refused. Static tokens accept `agents`, `agents_worker` and
  `agents_admin` after `;`, and `--oidc-agents-claim`,
  `--oidc-agents-worker-claim` and `--oidc-agents-admin-claim` grant the
  same capabilities from OIDC; no route checks them yet.
