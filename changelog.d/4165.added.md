- **The operator runs a `RavelCluster` on Azure Blob Storage with AKS
  workload identity.** `spec.storage.azure` takes `account`, `container`,
  `clientId` and optional per-role `roleClientIds`; exactly one of
  `storage.s3` and `storage.azure` must be set. Each tier and the qualify Job
  run as their own ServiceAccount annotated with
  `azure.workload.identity/client-id`, with `--store azure` and no stored
  credential. The cluster reports `BucketProtectionVerified=False`
  (`NotImplementedForAzure`) until a container protection check exists.
