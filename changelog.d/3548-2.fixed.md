- **The scrub, `ravel-cli catalog verify` and `ravel-cli load` charge their
  snapshot part decodes to the memory budget.** Each part's declared decoded
  size is reserved after its tenant check and before the decode, and held
  while the reader holds the part's entries. A refused reservation skips the
  scrub's covering-postings or seal-divergence check for that tick, logged and
  retried on the next tick, and fails `catalog verify` and the load's coverage
  check with a typed error. The CLI's budget is unlimited, so it does not
  refuse there.
