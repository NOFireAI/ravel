- **Sums, averages and not-equal counts answer from unfolded stamps.** On a
  logs table, `SUM(col)`, `SUM(col + k)`, `AVG(col)` over a declared `I64`
  column and `COUNT(*) WHERE col <> lit` now answer from the declared-column
  stamp on each segment's commit record when it carries the ADR-4628 sum and
  zero count, with no data-object GET and no fold needed. A segment whose
  stamp lacks them falls back to a `.cstat` entry the stamp grants, and with
  neither the statement scans.
