- **An hour sealed early by `--writers-stopped` or `--fold-after-load` stays
  open to late commits until its natural seal.** Every fold, the scheduled
  fold included, re-lists each hour above its own seal margin hour and at or
  below the HEAD watermark, one LIST per shard, and folds any commit the
  snapshot does not hold, so a commit another writer publishes into such an
  hour is visible to queries without a commit token from the next fold
  instead of staying invisible until a HEAD rebuild. A load whose hours
  another fold sealed while it was writing now succeeds with every commit in
  the snapshot, as long as its own fold runs before those hours' natural
  seal. A commit published after the natural seal is still invisible until a
  HEAD rebuild. A fold over a HEAD no operator-asserted seal raised issues no
  extra request.
