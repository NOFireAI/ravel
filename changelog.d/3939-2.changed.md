- The tier-2 key-index router's leaf GET ceiling now scales with the key set, `1,024 x (1 + min(k, 64))` for `k` keys (2,048 for a single key, as before), so a 100-key `IN` list over 30 logs day parts is covered at tier 2 instead of sending 14 days to the tier-1 probe; a part whose worst-case leaf GETs exceed twice its entry count is left to tier 1 without a leaf GET and counted in `partsUncovered`.
