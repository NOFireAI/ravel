- **PromQL selector-cache cursors skip the walk on a far jump and are built
  only when needed** (ADR-3072 decision 1, per-step cursor amendment, issue
  #3150). A slice bound that lies more than 64 samples past its cursor, as
  at a grid step much longer than the sample interval, is now found by one
  test of the sample 64 past the cursor and a binary search over the rest,
  instead of a 64-sample walk followed by the search. Cursors are allocated
  by the first slice narrower than the read's window, so an uncached read
  and the per-step reads of a key that fell back allocate none. Query
  results are unchanged.
