- **`--agents` now serves the step, effect and reconcile routes** (issue
  #3349). Workers journal each tool call with `worker/step` and report its
  attempt with `worker/effects/complete` or `worker/effects/fail`; a
  replacement worker's replay follows the step's recovery policy, a result
  for a superseded attempt answers `late: true` and is kept as evidence,
  and a `manual` effect in doubt blocks the turn with 409
  `effect_in_doubt`. A failed tool call is kept as a typed failure: the
  step stays open, its replay and `GET effects/{effect}` carry `failure`,
  and a non-terminal failed turn replays it on the next turn. Clients list
  and inspect effects with `GET effects` and `GET effects/{effect}`, and an
  `agents_admin` caller settles an effect in doubt with `reconcile`, which
  writes one `agents_reconcile` audit record per change through the audit
  pipeline. A
  `retry` hands the new attempt to the owner's next `worker/step` instead
  of dispatching it from the reconcile request. An `events` request
  with `limit` 0 is now 422 instead of reading one event.
