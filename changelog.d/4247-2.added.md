- **`compat certify` and its late-write pass retire a class-A reader against durable evidence.** `certify` runs a strict census of every live member object of a format family, holds the certificate behind the activation and per-bucket `G_pre` time gates, and refuses on any member below the target version or any member this build cannot read. Its census records its start time `S` in a resumable cursor, so a run that resumes keeps the earlier `S` and the daily late-write pass still examines records that landed after the census began; that pass re-reads records modified since the monotone watermark and marks the certificate contradicted on a lower-version find. The RLOG retention holds now also keep an expired object whose nested POSTINGS or KEY_IDX section version this build cannot read, not only one whose trailer version is out of range (ADR-4233 decision 7, hazard H5).
