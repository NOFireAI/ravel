- **A resident memory gate samples jemalloc and purges at a high-water mark.**
  Each `all`, `query` and `maintain` process runs a
  `ravel-memory-gate` thread that reads jemalloc `stats.resident` every
  `--memory-gate-interval-ms` (default 100). At or above
  `--memory-gate-high-water-percent` of the memory budget (default 70) it
  forces a purge of every arena's dirty and muzzy pages, restores their decay
  settings, re-reads, and closes the gate if the reading is still at or above
  the mark; a closed gate refuses memory reservations until a reading falls
  below it. Startup refuses a mark that the cache hard caps plus, in `all`
  mode, a bounded `--max-ingest-buffer-bytes` reach, naming both figures. The
  gate is off in `gateway` mode, with a fallback-sized budget, under
  `--disable-memory-gate`, and without jemalloc; the `memory gate resolved`
  startup line says which. `--memory-gate-wait-ms` is resolved and stamped
  but not yet read. New `/metrics` families: `ravel_memory_gate_resident_bytes`,
  `ravel_memory_gate_high_water_bytes`, `ravel_memory_gate_purges_total`,
  `ravel_memory_gate_samples_total`,
  `ravel_memory_gate_epoch_refresh_seconds_total`,
  `ravel_memory_gate_purge_seconds`, and `ravel_memory_gate_refusals_total`
  and `ravel_memory_budget_refusals_total` by `site`.
