- **`ravel-server` follows the `sys/compat` format write policy.** On a keyed
  bucket every process, in every mode, reads `sys/compat` at startup and every
  60 seconds. The alert evaluator writes alert history at the RLOG version
  the record names, refuses its writes on a stale view, and keeps the alert
  lease parked once `rlog` is activated. The catalog fold refuses its cycle,
  writing nothing, when a catalog family is active at a version it does not
  write. After an activation, a first touch creates a new bucket's
  provisioning record born fenced. `/metrics` renders
  `ravel_compat_publish_refused_total{family,reason}`,
  `ravel_compat_refresh_failures_total`, `ravel_compat_view_age_seconds`,
  `ravel_compat_active_write_version{family}` and `ravel_compat_epoch`
  (ADR-4233).
