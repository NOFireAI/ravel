- **The cache's S3-FIFO ghost window covers 30% of the cache in bytes**
  (ravel-cache, issue #3270). A key evicted from probation is remembered
  while it is inside the old key window (`max_bytes / max_entry_bytes` keys)
  or while the ghost's evicted sizes sum to at most 30% of `max_bytes`,
  whichever is wider. Before, at a 64 MiB entry cap the window was about 100
  keys, so a repeated statement reading more than the probation queue in
  1.3 MB ranges was never served from the cache: in the ClickBench hot sweep
  simulation q31, q32 and q33 now serve their second and third timed calls
  in full, where they served nothing. Both tiers use the policy. A hot set
  under a cold scan (ADR-0046 decision 6) and loops larger than the cache
  are served at least as before in the simulation. The ghost's key count is
  capped at one per mille of `max_bytes` in ghost bookkeeping.
