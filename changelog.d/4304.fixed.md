- **Maintenance holds more out-of-window inputs instead of failing, and
  activation can no longer read as fenced before its walk runs.** Compacting
  an RLOG input whose POSTINGS or KEY_IDX section is at a version this build
  does not read now holds the bucket (`HeldOutOfWindow`) instead of failing
  it, and the hold, the `compact-bucket` text and the `compact-tenant` line
  (` section=<name>`) name the section. The force 2 re-encode holds an RLOG
  or RSPAN part outside the reader window as `input_out_of_window`, as it
  already did for RSEG, and a part whose bytes do not match its record's
  content hash is held as `corrupt_input` rather than reported as a
  compaction invariant breach.
  Buckets that `migrate` holds no longer spend its budget, and a budget stop
  that made no progress still persists the walk position. `compat activate`
  now stamps an activation inside its CAS, one past the
  `fence_complete_unix_ns` it replaces, and a fence walk records
  `fence_complete_unix_ns` only while its epoch is still pending, so a late
  walker of an earlier epoch cannot mark the next activation fenced. The
  activation report carries `stamp_ahead_of_clock_ns` when that stamp is
  past the local clock by more than the clock skew allowance.
