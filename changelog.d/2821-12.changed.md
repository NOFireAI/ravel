- **MetricsBench corpus moves to format version 4: entries that sum floats
  across series carry a ULP tolerance** (issue #2821). `mb_selective_job_rate_sum`
  (2^18), `mb_selective_region_avg` (2^19), `mb_selective_error_ratio`,
  `mb_fanout_total_rate` and `mb_full_range_counter_increase` (2^20 each) now
  declare `tolerance_ulps`, each derived in its `modified` reason from the
  largest series count it sums on any profile: n-1 ULPs per sum of n
  non-negative values, plus the inner `rate`/`increase` roundings and one per
  division, doubled for two engines and rounded up to a power of two. The
  nightly ci run measured gaps of 1 to 4 ULPs on Ravel and Mimir for these
  entries, which read as `incorrect` before; VictoriaMetrics' gaps of 4.8e14
  ULPs and more still do. Single-series entries stay bit-exact, and a
  version-3 corpus file is refused.
