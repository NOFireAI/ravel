- **The decoded column-block cache refuses an admission that would evict a
  more used entry** (epic #3231, ADR-3326 LIKE-deferred amendment, decision
  1). Each entry and ghost record carries an access count saturating at 15;
  when an admission has to evict, an unreferenced victim counted at least as
  often as the newcomer refuses it
  (`the_gate_refuses_a_less_used_newcomer_and_ties`), and counts halve every
  `max(65,536, 10 x records)` lookups
  (`counts_halve_when_lookups_reach_the_aging_window`). A repeated scan
  larger than the cap now keeps a stable subset resident where CLOCK alone
  kept none
  (`an_over_cap_loop_keeps_a_stable_subset`). Refusals are reported as
  `frequencyRefusals` in a SQL response's `stats.decodedCache` and as
  `ravel_decoded_cache_frequency_refusals_total` on `/metrics`
  (`decoded_cache_stats_appear_once_when_enabled_and_never_at_zero`). The
  pressure evictor ignores counts
  (`the_pressure_evictor_frees_the_shortfall_plus_slack_whatever_the_counts`).
