- **A writer refuses to publish below a bucket's recorded format floor.** An
  ingest flush, a `maintain` process's compaction or erasure rewrite, or a
  `ravel-cli maintain migrate` run that would write an object below the
  tenant's recorded floor for its format family is refused
  before any PUT and counted under `ravel_format_floor_refused_total{family}`;
  a refused flush drops its rows and answers a strict write with HTTP 503. At
  startup each ingest and `maintain` process warns once per bucket of its
  `--tenant-token`, `--tenant-token-file` and `--maintain-tenant` tenants
  whose floor is above what it
  writes, and sets `ravel_format_floor_above_writer{family}`; it starts either
  way. `--write-below-format-floor` writes those objects instead, counts each
  under `ravel_format_floor_writes_below_total{family}`, and leaves the floor
  `contradicted` for `ravel-cli maintain audit-versions` (ADR-1746).
