- **The MetricsBench stack runs s3s-fs `0.17.0-ravel.2`** (issue #2821). It
  is `ravel.1` plus `TCP_NODELAY` on every accepted connection. `ravel.1` held
  each response's body until the client acknowledged its head, which added
  a 40 ms delayed-ACK stall to every query that touched storage on the `ci`
  stack (issue #3561). `run.sh` now downloads `ravel.2` and checks it against
  its sha256s. Figures measured on `ravel.1` are not compared with figures
  measured on `ravel.2`. The ClickBench entry still runs `ravel.1`.
