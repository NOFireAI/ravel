- **The MetricsBench cardinality read-back survives a 1,000,000-series
  answer and says where its time went** (issue #2821). `metricsbench_ingest`
  counts the `/api/v1/series` answer as it streams instead of holding the
  whole body, still refusing an answer whose `status` is not `success`; takes
  `--readback-timeout-secs` (default 30) for read-back queries, separate from
  the write timeout `--http-timeout-secs`; and records each query's wall time
  in `readback.query_ms`. `deploy/metricsbench/run.sh` passes the read-back
  timeout per profile (ci 30, cardinality 900, history 300, churn 900) and
  copies RustFS's `/logs` into `rustfs-logs/` before tearing the stack down.
  Every long-running service in the stack, ravel-server and RustFS included,
  now runs with `nofile` 524288 soft and hard, Docker's hard default, which
  the Go engines already raised themselves to.
