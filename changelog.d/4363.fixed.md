- **A flush held closed on a provisioning view the router cannot trust is
  dropped and counted after `max_flush_lifetime` instead of held without
  bound.** While the provisioning record could not be re-read, a buffer whose
  flush could not confirm its shard index was in the read-side scan set kept
  its rows, strict waiters and byte-budget charge for the whole outage. The
  hold now ends `max_flush_lifetime` after the first attempt that found no
  trusted view: the rows are not written, strict writers get a retryable 503,
  the bytes are refunded, the drop is logged at ERROR and counted on
  `ravel_ingest_abandoned_stale_provisioning_total` by signal. A shutdown
  inside that bound still writes the buffer in place. A flush waiting out a
  stale `sys/compat` view now asks it a last time one second before its
  deadline, so a view that answers then leaves the attempt time to publish.
